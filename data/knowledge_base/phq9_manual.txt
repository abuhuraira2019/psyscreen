PHQ-9: PATIENT HEALTH QUESTIONNAIRE - 9 ITEM
CLINICAL ADMINISTRATION MANUAL
============================================

OVERVIEW:
The PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9) is a validated, 9-item self-report depression screening tool developed by Dr. Robert Spitzer, Janet Williams, and Kurt Kroenke. It is based on the nine diagnostic criteria for Major Depressive Disorder from the DSM-IV. The PHQ-9 is one of the most widely used depression screening instruments in primary care and research settings.

ADMINISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS:
- The PHQ-9 covers the past TWO WEEKS only.
- Introduce with: "Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by any of the following problems?"
- Ask questions ONE AT A TIME and wait for patient response before proceeding.
- Validate the patient's answer briefly before moving to the next question.
- Response options must be presented as: Not at all (0) / Several days (1) / More than half the days (2) / Nearly every day (3)

THE 9 QUESTIONS (Ask in this exact order, one at a time):
---------------------------------------------------------
Question 1: "Little interest or pleasure in doing things?"
Question 2: "Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless?"
Question 3: "Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much?"
Question 4: "Feeling tired or having little energy?"
Question 5: "Poor appetite or overeating?"
Question 6: "Feeling bad about yourself — or that you are a failure or have let yourself or your family down?"
Question 7: "Trouble concentrating on things, such as reading the newspaper or watching television?"
Question 8: "Moving or speaking so slowly that other people could have noticed? Or the opposite — being so fidgety or restless that you have been moving around a lot more than usual?"
Question 9: "Thoughts that you would be better off dead or of hurting yourself in some way?"

FUNCTIONAL IMPAIRMENT ITEM (Optional follow-up after Q9):
"If you checked off any problems, how difficult have these problems made it for you to do your work, take care of things at home, or get along with other people?"
Options: Not difficult at all / Somewhat difficult / Very difficult / Extremely difficult

SCORING RULES:
--------------
Each item is scored: Not at all = 0, Several days = 1, More than half the days = 2, Nearly every day = 3
Total score range: 0 to 27

SEVERITY THRESHOLDS:
- Score 0–4:   Minimal or No Depression (Monitor; may not require treatment)
- Score 5–9:   Mild Depression (Watchful waiting; repeat PHQ-9 at follow-up)
- Score 10–14: Moderate Depression (Treatment plan, consider counseling, follow up)
- Score 15–19: Moderately Severe Depression (Active treatment with psychotherapy and/or pharmacotherapy)
- Score 20–27: Severe Depression (Immediate initiation of pharmacotherapy and, if severe impairment, referral to psychiatric specialist)

CRITICAL SAFETY RULE - QUESTION 9:
If the patient answers "Several days," "More than half the days," or "Nearly every day" to Question 9 (thoughts of self-harm), this requires IMMEDIATE clinical attention regardless of total score. Activate Crisis Protocol immediately.

ROMAN URDU SCORING GUIDE:
- "bilkul nahi" = Not at all (0)
- "kuch dinon mein" = Several days (1)
- "adhe se zyada din" = More than half the days (2)
- "har roz" or "hamesha" = Nearly every day (3)

DISCLAIMER:
The PHQ-9 is a screening tool, not a diagnostic instrument. A definitive diagnosis of depression must be made by a qualified healthcare professional who considers the full clinical history, severity, duration, and functional impact of symptoms. This tool is for research and educational purposes only.
