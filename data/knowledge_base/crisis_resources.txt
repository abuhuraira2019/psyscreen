CRISIS PROTOCOL — IMMEDIATE RESPONSE REQUIRED
==============================================
If a user expresses suicidal ideation, self-harm thoughts, or a wish to die, the following protocol MUST be activated immediately:

1. STOP all test administration immediately.
2. Respond with warmth and WITHOUT judgment.
3. Clearly acknowledge the user's distress.
4. Provide helpline numbers immediately.
5. Do NOT continue the mental health screening until the user confirms they are safe.
6. Recommend immediate human professional contact.

PAKISTAN MENTAL HEALTH HELPLINES:
===================================
1. UMANG PAKISTAN (Umang - Counseling Helpline)
   Phone: 0317-4288665
   Hours: Monday-Saturday, 9am-9pm
   Services: Confidential telephone counseling, crisis support, referrals
   Speciality: Urdu/Roman Urdu support available

2. ROZAN COUNSELING HELPLINE
   Phone: 051-2890505
   Location: Islamabad/Rawalpindi based but national reach
   Services: Counseling, mental health support

3. RESCUE 1122 (Emergency Services Pakistan)
   Phone: 1122
   Availability: 24/7
   Services: General emergency, can respond to mental health crises

4. EDHI FOUNDATION
   Phone: 115
   Services: General emergency services and welfare support

5. PAKISTAN INSTITUTE OF LIVING AND LEARNING (PILL)
   Website: https://pill.org.pk
   Services: Provides training for mental health professionals; referral directory

6. TASKEEN (Mental Health Platform Pakistan)
   Website: https://taskeen.org
   Services: Online therapy, awareness, crisis support

7. UMANG HELPLINE (Sindh)
   Phone: 0800-22444 (Sindh-specific)
   Services: Free helpline for mental health

SAFETY ASSESSMENT QUESTIONS (For Clinical Context Only):
=========================================================
When crisis is detected, a human clinician should assess:
1. Have you had thoughts of suicide or self-harm?
2. Do you have a plan? What is it?
3. Do you have access to the means (e.g., medications, weapons)?
4. Have you made any attempts before?
5. Do you have any reasons to live? What are they?
6. Is there someone with you right now?

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:
This AI system is NOT a crisis counselor. If a user expresses suicidal ideation or self-harm intent, the system must immediately provide emergency resources and STRONGLY encourage contact with a human professional. The AI system MUST NOT attempt to counsel the user through a suicidal crisis independently.
