ROMAN URDU / URDU CLINICAL SYMPTOM MAPPING CORPUS
PsyScreen-RAG Linguistic Processing Layer
For PHQ-9, GAD-7, and CBT Interventions
==============================================

PURPOSE:
This corpus maps common Roman Urdu (Minglish) and Urdu expressions to their standardized English clinical equivalents for accurate symptom interpretation and scoring.

=== CRISIS KEYWORDS (IMMEDIATE SAFETY PROTOCOL REQUIRED) ===
These words/phrases MUST trigger the crisis safety protocol:
- khudkushi (خودکشی) → SUICIDAL IDEATION / CRISIS
- khud ko nuqsaan (خود کو نقصان) → SELF-HARM
- marna chahta hoon (مرنا چاہتا ہوں) → SUICIDAL IDEATION (male)
- marna chahti hoon (مرنا چاہتی ہوں) → SUICIDAL IDEATION (female)
- zindagi khatam karna (زندگی ختم کرنا) → SUICIDAL IDEATION
- jina nahi chahta/chahti → PASSIVE SUICIDAL IDEATION
- apne aap ko hurt karna → SELF-HARM
- maut ki tamanna (موت کی تمنا) → WISH FOR DEATH
- dunya se chala jana chahta/chahti hoon → WISH TO DISAPPEAR/DIE
- khud ko takleef dena chahta hoon → SELF-HARM IDEATION
- life khatam karna chahta hoon → SUICIDAL IDEATION

- "dil nahi lagta" → Can't engage/no interest

=== RESPONSE OPTIONS IN ROMAN URDU (For all PHQ-9 and GAD-7 items) ===
- "bilkul nahi" / "nahi bilkul" → Not at all (Score: 0)
- "kuch baar" / "kuch dinon" / "thoda" / "kbhi kbhi" → Several days (Score: 1)
- "adhe se zyada time" / "zyada tar" / "aksar" → More than half the days (Score: 2)
- "hamesha" / "har roz" / "baar baar" / "roz" → Nearly every day (Score: 3)
